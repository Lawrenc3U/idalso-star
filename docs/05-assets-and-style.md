# Stage 5 — Style guide and asset manifest

## Direction

Chunky friendly workshop prison: sandstone, teal painted metal, warm orange clothing, rounded readable silhouettes. Avoid realistic weapons, copied uniforms, branded characters or distressing incarceration details. One stud is approximately 0.28 m for planning; fit actual assets against Roblox avatars in Studio.

Palette: sandstone #D8C092, teal #227784, orange #DE8B52, indigo #1B2A3E, mint #50DFA4, chalk #DCE0D1, gold #EDB54E. Use ore shape/pattern variation alongside colour. UI uses GothamBold, large buttons and high-contrast labels. Keep interactable shapes simpler than scenery.

Lighting implemented: 14:00, brightness 2, cool ambient, Atmosphere density .2, Bloom intensity .15/threshold 1.5. Streaming is enabled in the generated place. Depth of field, sun rays, heavy particles and screen shake are off. Select current supported lighting quality settings in Studio and compare on mobile; API/lighting terminology may change.

## Performance art gate

Start with the generated 108 blocks/mine, at most 2,808 across 26 mines, plus simple environment parts. Do not increase dimensions until measuring. Fixed blocks are anchored. Reset creation yields between layers. Initial target: 30 FPS on a chosen low-end mobile device at eight players, without sustained reset stalls. Measure actual draw calls, memory, render/physics timings and replication; these are unverified budgets. Disable cosmetic effects to compare. Replace detail with atlases instead of stacking many tiny parts.
